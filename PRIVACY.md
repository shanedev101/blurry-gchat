# 🔒 Privacy Policy for Shroudly

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

**Shroudly** ("the Extension") is a browser extension developed by **shanedev101**. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us, and the Extension is designed from the ground up to protect your data.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection

The Extension operates **entirely locally** within your web browser sandbox.

- We **do not collect, store, transmit, or share** any of your personal data, chat messages, contact names, profile pictures, or browsing history.
- The Extension contains **no analytics trackers, cookies, advertising scripts, or logging utilities**.

---

## 2. Local Storage Usage

To function, the Extension requests the `storage` permission.

- This is used solely to store your custom settings (such as blur intensity, toggle states, and keybind preferences) on your local device.
- This data never leaves your computer and is not uploaded to any remote servers.

---

## 3. Network Connectivity

The Extension **does not make any network requests** to external servers. It operates completely offline, except for native communications inside your Google Chat tab (`https://chat.google.com/*`) as defined by the host permissions.

---

## 4. Open Source Transparency

To guarantee security and verify this privacy statement, the entire codebase of Shroudly is open-source and available for audit under the MIT License.

---

## 5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our official repository.
