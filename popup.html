Backup

Export downloads a JSON file with all your settings, section layout, and saved threads (aliases, pins, tags).

Clear all data?

This permanently removes all settings, the section layout, and every saved thread (aliases, pins, tags), restoring factory defaults. This cannot be undone.

Disable Shroudly?

All blurring and Thread Manager decorations will stop until you turn it back on. Your saved settings and threads are kept.
